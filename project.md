## Project Grading

The project is **30%** of the course grade, split as follows.

| Deliverable | Share of project grade | Share of course grade |
|---|---|---|
| Reports 1–4 | 5% each (20% total) | 1.5% each (6% total) |
| Report 5 – Final project report | 50% | 15% |
| Video | 15% | 4.5% |
| Build | 15% | 4.5% |
| **Total** | **100%** | **30%** |

**Report 5.** The final report is limited to **10 pages**. The title page, table of contents and appendix do not count toward the limit.

- Put every table, spec sheet and other reference you cite in the appendix.
- If needed, you can move FEA figures to the appendix. Keep the critical points and main results in the body of the report.
- Include an appendix with an AI-generated GD&T analysis, clearly labeled as AI-generated.

For full credit, among other things:

- justify every design decision, referring back to standard sizes and spec sheets;
- clearly identify the critical locations;
- static analysis with a safety factor;
- yield analysis with a safety factor;
- FEA analysis: describe the applied forces and the supports/boundary conditions, show the mesh refinement, and include a convergence study;
- safety factors for the additional components;
- clarity of analysis: present every analysis so it is easy to follow.

**Video.** Explain the key points of your report and how they relate to the actual build. The video is graded on communication: focus on the important parts, choose the essential elements, and do not read a script of non-essentials. Present it the way you would present the project in a design interview.

**Build.** 10% of the project grade (3% of the course grade) for the machine moving as it should, and 5% (1.5% of the course grade) for creativity, going beyond the basic requirements.
